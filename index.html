Why I built it

For years, I wanted a tool that helped me dream — and actually come back to those dreams.

Somewhere along the way, I noticed something about myself and the people around me: we were dreaming less than we used to.

Days fill up. Calendars fill up. And the big questions — Where is my family heading? What do I want for my health, my career, my kids? — quietly get pushed to “someday.”

But dreaming isn’t a luxury. It’s critical. A clear vision for your life, your family, your health, your career and your kids is what turns busy days into meaningful ones.

As a family, we would fly to self-leadership conferences just to make space for this — to build vision boards, clarify what we wanted, and meet like-minded people doing the same.

Those few days were powerful. Then we’d fly home, the vision board would go in a drawer, and everyday life took over again.

So I built Dreamly. An iPhone app with one job: help you dream — and keep your dreams in front of you.

How it works

From a dream, to a plan, to the next step.

🌅

Visions

A vision is a picture of the future you’re building, in one area of life: life, family, health, wealth, career, kids, your team or company — or an area of your own. Describe it in your own words, add the quotes that keep you going, and pin pictures to it.

🖼

Vision boards

Every vision has its own vision board — the pictures that make that future feel real. Your dream home. The trip. The finish line.

Make boards of your own too — “Dream home”, “2027 travel” — mixing pictures however you like.

And Dreamly combines all your visions into one board automatically: your whole future, on one screen.

🔁

Review

A vision you never look at is just a wish. Reviewing your visions regularly is what keeps them alive — daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly or on dates you choose.

Dreamly reminds you when a review is due, and one Review button walks you through each vision, its pictures and quotes, with room to jot down what moved you closer and what’s next.

💡

Ideas

Ideas arrive anywhere — in the shower, on a walk, scrolling LinkedIn or X. Capture them in seconds by typing, by voice, or by sharing a post straight into Dreamly.

An idea can stand on its own, or be tied to the vision it serves.

✅

Steps

An idea only matters once you act on it. Break it into steps with target dates and notes, watch your progress, and get a gentle nudge to keep going.

🔒 Your dreams stay yours — stored on your device, no ads, no tracking, nothing sold.
