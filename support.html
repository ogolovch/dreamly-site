Capture Ideas and Vision
What's the difference between a vision and an idea?

A vision is where you're headed — for your life, health, wealth, career, a project, your team, your company or your kids. An idea is something you might do. Link ideas to the vision they serve.

How do I save a post from LinkedIn, X, Reddit, Facebook or Instagram?

Tap Share in that app, then Dreamly. Choose New idea or add it to an existing idea. Screenshots work the same way from Photos. If Dreamly isn't in the row, scroll to More and switch it on.

How do vision reviews work?

Each vision has its own rhythm: daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly or specific dates. Dreamly reminds you at your chosen time, and Review walks you through each vision, its quotes and pictures.

Can I dictate instead of typing?

Yes — tap the microphone next to any text field. Tap again to stop. If you see a permission message, allow Microphone and Speech Recognition in Settings ▸ Dreamly.

How does search work?

Search covers every word: titles, notes, visions, quotes, steps, step comments, comments, tags and links. Use "quotes" for a phrase, #tag, -word to exclude, and filters like is:progress, rank:high or has:link.

How do I share an idea?

Open it and tap ••• ▸ Share as text for anyone, or Send to a Dreamly user to send a file that opens in their app with its plan, links and pictures.

Will my data sync across devices?

Turn on iCloud in Settings. Backup keeps all text; Full sync includes pictures. You can also export a complete backup file at any time.

What does the one-time unlock include?

Your first 10 ideas and visions (together) are free — the examples you start with don't count. A single $2.99 purchase unlocks unlimited ideas and visions forever — no subscription. Changed phones? Tap Restore purchase.

How do tags and boards work?

Ideas and visions share one set of tags — plain words like Health, Leadership or Urgent; filter by them or search #leadership. Boards come in two families. From your visions: every vision has a board inside it, shown when you review it, and All visions combines them automatically. My boards are boards of their own (a dream home, a trip) — not tied to a vision, so they stay out of reviews. Use Arrange to drag pictures into order.

Can I use Siri, lock the app or take photos?

Yes. Say “Capture an idea in Dreamly” (or add it to the Action button in Shortcuts). Turn on Settings ▸ Privacy & security ▸ Lock with Face ID. Add pictures ▸ Take a photo for your vision board.
